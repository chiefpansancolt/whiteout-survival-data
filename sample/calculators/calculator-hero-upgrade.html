Hero Upgrade Calculator

Plan one hero at a time. Stars use the in-game label, so 3.1 is star 3 and tier 1, and 5.0 is the last label. Each step to the next label costs shards, and the costs are the same for every hero. A skill level costs the manuals of the hero rarity (Mythic for Legendary heroes), Exploration or Expedition by the kind of the skill, and a level can need a star. Widgets level the exclusive weapon of a Legendary hero from 0 to 10. Power is not included. Nothing is saved, and reloading the page clears your choices.
