Fire Crystal Shard Table Explorer

From fireCrystalShardTable(). Fire Crystal Shards and Refined Fire Crystals for T11 and T12 troop research. The Unlock row sums the research that unlocks the troop: tiers 1 to 5 for T11 and tier 1 for T12. The extras are the research after that, one row for each research tier. T11 costs no Refined Fire Crystals, so only T12 has that column. Pick T11, T12, or both. A dash means the column has no cost in that row.
