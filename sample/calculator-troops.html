Troop Calculator

Set the level of each camp and what it trains or promotes. The page adds up the troops you get by troop type and tier. All three camps share one training capacity, which is the capacity of the three camp levels added together plus the bonuses below. Nothing is saved, and reloading the page clears your choices.

Training adds troops at its tier. Promotion takes the troops from the first tier and adds them to the second, shown in red and green. Promotion to tier 12 uses the promotion cost of tier 12. A camp can do both, each with its own count and batches. The capacity of the levels from 30-1 up to Fire Crystal 10, and the cost and time of each troop, come from WoS Tools. Time is the time of one troop times the troops, divided by 1 plus the training speed, rounded down, for each batch. Enter your training speed without the buffs below it, as the game shows it.
