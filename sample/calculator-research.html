Research Calculator

For each research line, pick the level you have now and the level you want. The page adds up the resources and the time for the levels in between. Each tier of a research line is its own line here, with its own levels. Click a line in the tree to pick its levels, or use the list. Nothing is saved, and reloading the page clears your choices.

Enter your research speed as the game shows it, without the buffs here.
